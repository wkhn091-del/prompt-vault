# Harborlux — "Claim Your Summit" rainy-harbour penthouse hero

Build a single full-screen hero for a fictional luxury real-estate brand called HARBORLUX. On load it plays a 7.3-second cinematic intro, then stays live with a moving rain sky. In order: a pair of black lacquer doors slides open from the centre seam onto a top-down drone shot of a skyscraper rooftop pool at night. The camera tilts up and pulls back to reveal a rainy neon harbour skyline. The shot then turns into four separate image layers that drift at different speeds (parallax), while a giant white word "Summit" rises in letter by letter. Finally a man in a long dark coat rises into frame from below, standing at a glass balcony railing with his back to us, and his head overlaps the bottom of the giant word.
